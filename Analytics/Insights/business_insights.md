# Business Insights

## Q1. Revenue & Sales Trend

### Key Finding

Monthly revenue reached approximately **1.18M in November 2017** and **1.16M in April 2018**, indicating strong sales during the core growth period. Revenue then drops sharply toward the end of the observed period.

### Business Interpretation

The business experienced strong sales activity but shows a significant decline in the final observed months. This pattern should be investigated further to determine whether it reflects a genuine decline in demand or incomplete data for the final period.

---

## Q2. Product Category Performance

### Key Finding

**Health & Beauty** generated the highest revenue at approximately **1.44M**, followed by **Watches & Gifts (1.31M)** and **Bed, Bath & Table (1.24M)**. These categories also account for substantial order and item volumes.

### Business Interpretation

A relatively small group of categories contributes a significant portion of sales, making these categories important drivers of business performance.

### Recommended Action

Prioritize high-performing categories for inventory planning, promotions, and seller availability while identifying opportunities to grow promising lower-performing categories.

---

## Q3. Seller Performance

### Key Finding

The platform has **3,095 sellers** contributing to approximately **15.84M in total sales** across **112,650 items**. Seller performance varies considerably, with some sellers contributing substantially more sales than others.

### Business Interpretation

The broad seller base provides product variety, but the variation in seller contribution suggests that a smaller group of sellers may be particularly important to overall sales.

### Recommended Action

Identify high-performing sellers for retention and strategic partnerships while monitoring seller concentration to reduce dependency on a limited number of sellers.

---

## Q4. Customer Behavior

### Key Finding

Only **2,913 customers (3.1%)** were repeat customers, compared with **92,507 one-time customers**. However, repeat customers generated an average spend of **310.49**, nearly **1.9×** the **161.49** average spend of one-time customers.

### Business Interpretation

The customer base has strong potential for retention-driven growth. Although repeat customers represent a small share of customers, they are substantially more valuable on an average-spend basis.

### Recommended Action

Focus on retention strategies such as personalized offers, post-purchase engagement, and incentives for second purchases.

---

## Q5. Geographic Performance

### Key Finding

**São Paulo (SP)** is the dominant market, generating approximately **5.92M in revenue** from **41,375 orders**. Rio de Janeiro and Minas Gerais follow with approximately **2.13M** and **1.86M** in revenue respectively.

### Business Interpretation

Sales are highly concentrated in a few major states, with SP alone contributing a substantial share of overall revenue.

### Recommended Action

Maintain strong operational and marketing support in SP while exploring growth opportunities in RJ, MG, and other high-potential states.

---

## Q6. Delivery Performance

### Key Finding

Approximately **6.62% of orders were delivered late**, while the average delivery time was **12.5 days**. Among orders with delivery-delay data, the average delay was approximately **-11.88 days**, meaning orders were delivered earlier than their estimated dates on average.

### Business Interpretation

Overall delivery performance is relatively strong, but late-delivery rates vary significantly across states. Some regions experience considerably higher late-delivery rates than the overall average.

### Recommended Action

Investigate high late-delivery regions to identify logistics, fulfillment, or transportation constraints and improve regional delivery reliability.

---

## Q7. Customer Satisfaction

### Key Finding

Delivery performance shows a clear association with customer satisfaction. Average review scores were **4.29 for early deliveries**, **4.04 for on-time deliveries**, and only **2.27 for late deliveries**.

### Business Interpretation

Customers receiving orders late tend to provide substantially lower ratings than customers receiving orders on time or early. This highlights delivery reliability as an important part of the overall customer experience.

### Recommended Action

Reducing late deliveries should be treated not only as an operational goal but also as a customer-experience priority.

---

## Q8. Freight & Order Economics

### Key Finding

The platform generated approximately **15.84M in order value** and incurred **2.25M in freight costs**. Average freight was **22.82 per order**, while the average order-level freight-to-product-price ratio was **30.91%**.

### Business Interpretation

Freight represents a meaningful component of order economics, particularly when compared with product prices. High freight-to-price ratios may put additional pressure on the economics of lower-value orders.

### Recommended Action

Analyze shipping costs by region, product category, and order value to identify opportunities for reducing freight costs and improving order-level profitability.
